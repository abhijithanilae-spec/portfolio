// case study

The age-gate leak: +30% checkouts

High Meta click-through rates, but few Shopify checkouts. Where were people going?

Problem

Ads were getting clicks but the checkout count didn’t follow, and top-line reporting couldn’t explain the gap.

Method

Tagged every ad with UTM parameters, mapped them into a GA4 Funnel Exploration and joined the result with Shopify data.

Result

One creative’s visitors dropped off at the 21+ age-verification step. I paused it, moved budget to the creative driving verified transactions, and checkouts rose 30%.
